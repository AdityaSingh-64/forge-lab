Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What does a web development company do?

A web development company plans, designs, builds, tests, launches, and maintains websites and web applications. Work can include custom front ends, CMS platforms, integrations, eCommerce features, performance improvements, and technical SEO.

What types of websites can you develop?

ForgeLab develops business websites, corporate sites, landing pages, portfolio or brochure sites, eCommerce stores, CMS-powered websites, and custom web applications tailored to your goals and requirements.

Do you provide WordPress website development?

Yes. We create responsive, SEO-friendly WordPress websites with custom themes or page templates, content structures, integrations, performance work, and easy-to-use editing experiences.

Can you build Shopify and WooCommerce websites?

Yes. We can develop or customize Shopify and WooCommerce websites, including product presentation, cart and checkout experiences, payment setup, content management, and required integrations.

How do you choose the right technology?

We evaluate your goals, content, features, budget, integrations, team skills, scalability, and long-term maintenance needs. The best stack is the one that fits the project—not simply the most popular option.

How long does a website development project take?

The timeline depends on the scope, content, design, features, integrations, and approval cycles. After discovery, we provide a project plan with realistic milestones before development begins.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We build clean, semantic, mobile-friendly foundations with clear page structure, crawlable content, performance considerations, metadata, and technical SEO best practices.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can audit an existing website and redesign or rebuild it with a clearer structure, modern interface, improved mobile experience, better functionality, and stronger performance.

What does one year of free support include?

Every new ForgeLab website project includes one year of free support for agreed post-launch assistance, troubleshooting, and minor fixes. Hosting, third-party fees, major new features, and out-of-scope work may be charged separately.

Do you provide ongoing website maintenance?

Yes. After the included support period, we can provide ongoing maintenance, updates, security checks, performance monitoring, troubleshooting, backups, and feature development.
