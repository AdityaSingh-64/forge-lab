Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What does website maintenance and support include?

Website maintenance and support can include updates, bug fixes, security checks, backups, performance monitoring, compatibility testing, minor content and technical assistance, and coordination of hosting, domain, SSL, and email services where included in the plan.

Can you support a website that ForgeLab did not build?

Yes. We can often support websites developed by another team or agency. We first review the platform, technology, integrations, access, current condition, and support requirements so we can recommend a realistic care plan.

Which platforms and technologies do you support?

Our support can cover WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, custom PHP, React, Next.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, web applications, APIs, and common third-party integrations. We assess the technology first because the right maintenance approach depends on the stack.

Do you provide urgent or emergency support?

Yes, we can help assess and resolve urgent issues such as downtime, unexpected errors, compromised websites, failed deployments, and broken critical journeys. Availability and response times depend on the support plan agreed for your website.

Will you back up my website?

Backup support is available as part of an appropriate maintenance plan. The frequency, retention, storage, responsibilities, and restoration process are agreed after reviewing the website, database, hosting environment, and recovery requirements.

How is website maintenance priced?

Cost depends on the website's technology, size, complexity, update frequency, support expectations, hosting, third-party services, and the level of ongoing work you need. We review the website and recommend a plan with a clear scope before work begins.

What is the difference between care, support, and feature work?

Care covers planned maintenance such as updates, checks, backups, monitoring, and optimization. Support covers troubleshooting and agreed technical assistance. Feature work covers new functionality, integrations, redesigns, or larger improvements and is scoped separately.

Do you guarantee same-day or 24-hour support?

No blanket response-time guarantee is made. Response times and service availability depend on the selected support model, agreed scope, severity of the issue, and your access to the website and required providers. Any commitments are documented in the support agreement.

Can you help with hosting, domains, and email?

Yes, we can help coordinate hosting, domains, SSL certificates, email, and related technical services. The exact provider, billing, and support responsibilities are agreed separately from ForgeLab's design and development work.

What do you need to get started?

Contact us with the website URL, your support goals, any urgent issues, and access to the relevant code, CMS, hosting, domain, analytics, and third-party accounts. We will use that information to review the site and recommend the right plan.
