SEO built around how people actually search

Search engines reward websites that are fast, relevant, trustworthy, and genuinely useful. Everything we do is aimed at one of those four goals, and every recommendation is tied to a business outcome rather than a vanity metric.

We start with a full technical and content audit, identify the keywords your customers are actually using, and map each one to the right page. From there we work through the fixes that matter most, in order of impact.

Our SEO services

Technical SEO

We fix the foundations Google needs to crawl and index your site: sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical tags, redirect chains, broken links, structured data, Core Web Vitals, and mobile usability issues that quietly suppress rankings.

On-Page SEO

Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, image optimization, and keyword-aligned content for every page that matters — so each page has a clear purpose and a strong reason to rank.

Content & Keyword Strategy

Keyword research mapped to real search intent, then content built to answer it properly. We plan topical coverage across your whole site, not just isolated keywords, so you compete for the full set of searches that matter.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, local landing pages, citation consistency, review strategy, and local link building — designed to put you in front of customers who are ready to buy nearby.

Link Building

Earn relevant, authoritative links through digital PR, content that others cite, broken link reclamation, and partnerships. Quality and relevance matter far more than volume, and we never buy spam links.

Reporting & Analytics

Google Search Console and analytics tracking set up properly, with clear reporting on rankings, organic traffic, conversions, and revenue. You see what changed, why it changed, and what we are doing next.

How our SEO process works

01 — Audit

Establish your baseline

02 — Strategy

03 — Execute

Ship the fixes

04 — Measure

Track and iterate

We track rankings, traffic, and conversions monthly, refine what is working, and adjust the plan as results come in. SEO compounds, so month over month is where the real gains show up.

Frequently asked questions

What does an SEO agency do?

An SEO agency improves a website's visibility in organic search results. This includes technical fixes, keyword research, on-page optimization, content creation, link building, and ongoing performance tracking.

Which SEO services do you provide?

ForgeLab provides technical SEO, on-page SEO, content and keyword strategy, local SEO, link building, eCommerce SEO, and SEO reporting and analytics.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and timelines depend on factors such as your industry, competition, domain authority, current site health, and content depth. Technical and on-page improvements can show movement within weeks, while competitive rankings typically take several months.

How is SEO different from paid advertising?

Paid advertising buys visibility immediately and stops when spending stops. SEO builds organic rankings that continue to generate traffic and compound over time. Most businesses use both: paid ads for immediate results and SEO for sustainable long-term growth.

Do you offer local SEO?

Yes. Our local SEO services include Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword targeting, citation building, review strategy, location landing pages, and local link building to help businesses appear in nearby search results.

How do you measure SEO success?

We track organic traffic, keyword rankings, impressions and clicks in Google Search Console, conversions such as leads and sales, and revenue attributed to organic search. Reporting is tied to your business goals rather than vanity metrics.
