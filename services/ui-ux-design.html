06 — How we move

Our UI/UX Design Process

A practical, collaborative process that keeps decisions grounded in user needs and business goals.

01

Discover

We start by understanding your business, product, users, competitors, technology, and objectives. We identify the problems we’re solving and define what success should look like.

02

Research

We analyze your users, market, competitors, existing product, user behaviour, and industry patterns. The goal is to uncover opportunities before design begins.

03

Define

We translate research into a clear UX direction. This can include personas, user journeys, information architecture, feature priorities, and user flows.

04

Wireframe

We create low-fidelity wireframes to establish the structure and functionality of the experience. This allows ideas to be evaluated before visual details are introduced.

05

Design

We develop the final interface with typography, colour, spacing, imagery, components, interaction patterns, and responsive layouts. Every element is designed to work as part of the larger experience.

06

Prototype

We connect the screens into an interactive prototype that demonstrates the intended user journey and provides a realistic representation of how the product will behave.

07

Test

We review the experience for usability, consistency, accessibility, and potential friction points. Feedback is documented and translated into design improvements.

08

Handoff

We prepare the final designs and supporting documentation for development. Developers receive the necessary specifications, assets, components, states, and interaction details to accurately build the experience.

11 — Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a UI/UX design agency do?

A UI/UX design agency helps businesses create digital experiences that are intuitive, visually engaging, accessible, and aligned with user needs and business objectives. This can include user research, UX strategy, information architecture, wireframing, UI design, prototyping, usability testing, and design systems.

What’s the difference between UI and UX design?

UX design focuses primarily on the overall user experience — how users navigate, understand, and interact with a product. UI design focuses on the visual interface, including typography, colours, layouts, components, imagery, and visual consistency. Both work together to create a complete digital experience.

What UI/UX design services do you provide?

ForgeLab provides UX research, UX strategy, UX audits, information architecture, user flows, wireframing, UI design, website design, mobile app design, SaaS and dashboard design, e-commerce UX, design systems, interactive prototyping, usability testing, and developer handoff.

Do you design websites and mobile applications?

Yes. We design websites, web applications, mobile applications, SaaS platforms, dashboards, e-commerce experiences, and other digital products.

Can you redesign our existing website or application?

Yes. We can audit an existing experience, identify usability and visual issues, and redesign the product while considering your existing brand, technology, users, and business objectives.

Do you provide wireframes and prototypes?

Yes. Depending on the project, we can provide low-fidelity wireframes, high-fidelity UI designs, and interactive prototypes that demonstrate the complete user journey.

Do you provide design systems?

Yes. We can create scalable design systems containing reusable components, typography, colour systems, spacing rules, interaction states, and other design guidelines.

Do you work with developers during implementation?

Yes. We can prepare development-ready designs and specifications and collaborate with development teams to ensure the final implementation follows the intended design and interaction behaviour.

How long does a UI/UX design project take?

Project timelines depend on the product’s complexity, number of screens, research requirements, functionality, revisions, and project scope. A simple landing page may take considerably less time than a full SaaS platform or mobile application.

Do you provide customized UI/UX design packages?

Yes. We create project scopes based on your product, users, objectives, number of screens, research requirements, functionality, and overall design requirements rather than forcing every project into the same package.
