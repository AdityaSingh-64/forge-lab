Frequently asked questions

What does an eCommerce development company do?

An eCommerce development company designs, develops, integrates, launches, and maintains online stores and commerce platforms. This can include everything from Shopify and WooCommerce stores to custom eCommerce platforms and marketplaces.

How much does eCommerce website development cost?

The cost depends on factors such as the number of products, platform, design requirements, custom features, integrations, payment systems, and overall complexity. We recommend defining the scope before estimating the investment.

Which eCommerce platform should I choose?

There isn't one platform that's right for every business. Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom eCommerce development each have different strengths. We help you choose based on your products, business model, budget, technical requirements, and growth plans.

Do you provide Shopify development services?

Yes. We can help with Shopify store development, custom themes, customization, integrations, migration, optimization, and ongoing improvements.

Do you build WooCommerce websites?

Yes. We develop and customize WooCommerce stores with features such as product management, payment gateways, shipping integrations, customer accounts, and third-party integrations.

Can you migrate an existing online store?

Yes. We can help migrate your eCommerce store, including products, customer information, orders, content, and integrations, depending on the source and destination platforms.

Can you integrate payment gateways?

Yes. We can integrate payment gateways and other third-party services required for your eCommerce business.

Will my eCommerce website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. We follow a responsive and mobile-first approach to ensure customers can browse and purchase comfortably across smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

Can you help improve an existing eCommerce store?

Absolutely. We can audit an existing store and work on areas such as UX, performance, mobile experience, technical SEO, checkout flow, integrations, and new functionality.

Do you provide ongoing eCommerce support?

Yes. We can provide ongoing maintenance, technical support, security updates, performance optimization, troubleshooting, and feature development.
