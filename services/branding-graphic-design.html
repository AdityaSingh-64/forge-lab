Our branding process

A thoughtful path from discovery to launch

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with conversations. We learn about your business, audience, competitors, values, personality, challenges, and ambitions. The goal is to understand the story behind the brand before we start designing it.

  2. 02

    Research

    We study your market and competitive landscape to identify opportunities for differentiation. We look at what your audience expects — and where your brand can stand apart.

  3. 03

    Brand Strategy

    We define the strategic foundation of your brand, including positioning, personality, messaging direction, audience, and visual opportunities.

  4. 04

    Creative Direction

    We explore visual directions that could bring your brand to life through moodboards, visual references, typography, colour, imagery, and creative concepts.

  5. 05

    Design

    We develop the chosen direction into a complete visual identity. From your logo and colour palette to typography and supporting graphic elements, every piece works together.

  6. 06

    Refine

    Design improves through conversation. We review the identity with you, gather feedback, and refine the details until everything feels right.

  7. 07

    Brand Guidelines

    We document the identity so your team knows exactly how to use it — keeping your brand consistent as it grows.

  8. 08

    Launch

    Your new identity is ready to meet the world. We can help roll it out across your website, social media, marketing materials, packaging, presentations, and other brand touchpoints.

Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a branding agency do?

A branding agency helps businesses define, develop, and communicate their identity. This can include brand strategy, positioning, logo design, visual identity, messaging direction, brand guidelines, and supporting graphic design.

What is included in brand identity design?

A brand identity can include your logo, colour palette, typography, visual elements, imagery direction, layouts, iconography, and brand guidelines. The exact scope depends on your business requirements.

Do you provide logo design services?

Yes. We design custom logos and logo systems that are created to work across digital platforms, print, packaging, social media, and other brand applications.

Can you rebrand an existing business?

Yes. We can help businesses refresh or completely rethink their visual identity while considering existing brand equity, customers, market positioning, and future goals.

What is the difference between branding and graphic design?

Branding defines how your business is perceived and represented. Graphic design is one of the tools used to express that brand visually.
Branding establishes the system; graphic design brings that system to life across different materials and experiences.

Do you create brand guidelines?

Yes. We create brand guidelines that document how your logo, colours, typography, imagery, and other visual elements should be used.

Can you design social media creatives?

Yes. We create social media templates, campaign graphics, advertisements, banners, and other branded digital content.

Do you design packaging?

Yes. We can create packaging concepts and visual systems designed around your product, audience, brand identity, and market.

Can you work with an existing brand identity?

Absolutely. We can work within your existing brand guidelines or help improve and expand your current visual system.

Do you also provide website and digital marketing services?

Yes. ForgeLab can support branding, UI/UX design, web development, and digital marketing, making it easier to maintain a consistent brand experience across your digital presence.
